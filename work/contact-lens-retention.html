Charting a new experience for contact lens wearers

UX Designer & Researcher · Whipsaw · Mar–Aug 2023, ongoing engagement
30%
of new contact lens wearers don't make it past the first 30 days of use
7%
make it to a full year of use
6%
annual growth in global vision-correction need

A contact lens industry that hadn't meaningfully changed in twenty years was failing to educate and support new wearers at exactly the moments they needed it most.

From scoping the project proposal to delivering the final concept, I guided the team through a participatory-design-informed process to uncover unmet needs and develop product concepts that resonate with new wearers globally.

Process

01
Discover — Opportunity area definition
  • Synthesized five years of prior research on new-wearer pain points to target the areas worth exploring
  • Ran a full-day, on-site stakeholder workshop using "Project in a Day" — walking client and design teams through the entire project scope together to build cross-functional alignment
  • Surfaced three guiding themes: set expectations before the first visit, give eye care providers better tools, deliver education where the patient already is
02
Dream — Co-creation workshops
  • Held three rounds of co-creation workshops with new contact lens wearers and eye care professionals
  • Ran paired ideation sessions where participants and designers built solutions to top needs together, not for them
  • Used the sessions to hold both the big-picture product vision and the granular feature decisions at once
03
Develop — User flows & qualitative testing
  • Defined a patient app experience spanning first-appointment booking, expectation-setting, interactive training, short-form video education, and telemedicine
  • Refined feature positioning through iterative wireframing, prototyping, and qualitative testing with end users and eye care professionals
